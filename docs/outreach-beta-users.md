## Message 2: Grid connection consultant

**Target companies:** Roadnight Taylor, EnergyNet, G2 Energy, GridBeyond

**Subject:** Tool that might save your team time on initial grid screening

**Message:**

Hi [Name],

I've built a platform called Princeps that pulls live data from all 6 UK DNOs (NGED CKAN, UKPN/SSEN/SPEN/WPD via OpenDataSoft, NESO TEC register) and runs automated grid connection assessments — Tier 1 instant screening plus Tier 2 pandapower simulations.

It generates a professional Grid Connection Report PDF with:
- Nearest 5 substations with headroom, voltage, RAG status
- P10/P50/P90 connection costs across 11kV/33kV/132kV options
- Queue depth analysis from ECR/TEC data
- Power flow results (voltage deviations, thermal loading, N-1 contingency)
- Flexible connection / ANM comparison

I'm not trying to replace your expertise — the opposite. I think this could be a tool your team uses to do initial screening faster, so you spend your time on the complex cases that need human judgement.

Would you be up for a 15-minute demo? I'll run a site you choose and you can tell me if the output is credible.

Best,
Anya

## Where to find contacts

1. **LinkedIn** — Search "[Company] + Development Manager" or "Head of Grid" or "Land & Property"
2. **Company websites** — Most list their team on /about or /team pages
3. **Conference attendee lists** — Solar & Storage Live, All-Energy, Renewables UK
4. **Shared connections** — Ask existing network for warm intros
