1. Reliance, scope and methodology

Reliance statement

This report is prepared for the applicant ({{ inp.site_name }}) for use in discussions with {{ inp.lpa or "the Local Planning Authority" }} regarding the financial viability of the proposed scheme under the National Planning Policy Framework (December 2024) and the Planning Practice Guidance (Viability, ID 10). No third party may rely on this report without prior written consent. Princeps accepts no liability to any third party for any matter arising from the content.

1.1 Purpose

This Financial Viability Assessment (FVA) tests whether the proposed {{ inp.capacity_mw }} MW {{ inp.technology }} scheme at {{ inp.site_name }} can sustain the package of planning obligations (s106 community benefit, CIL, BNG delivery, habitat management, landscape mitigation) that the LPA is asking, while still generating a residual land value sufficient to secure the land from a reasonable willing landowner.

1.2 Standards applied

  • RICS Professional Standard — Financial Viability in Planning: Conduct and Reporting. 1st ed. 2019, 2nd ed. effective 1 May 2021. Sections 2 (scope), 3 (BLV), 4 (appraisal) and 5 (reporting) apply.
  • RICS Valuation — Global Standards (Red Book Global), effective 31 January 2025. VPS 1 (terms of engagement), VPS 3 (valuation approach), VPS 4 (applications).
  • NPPF (Dec 2024) Annex 2 (Glossary — viability) and para. 58 (decision-taking — viability of plan-making level obligations in decision-taking).
  • Planning Practice Guidance — Viability (ID 10); Planning Obligations (ID 23b); Community Infrastructure Levy (ID 25).

1.3 Method

Residual appraisal: the scheme's Gross Development Value (GDV) is calculated by income capitalisation of projected PPA revenue over the PPA term, with total costs (CAPEX, opex PV, policy costs, and developer profit benchmark) deducted. The Residual Land Value (RLV) is then tested against the Benchmark Land Value (BLV) derived per RICS FVP 2021 §3.

BLV = EUV + premium, tested against market evidence (comparable transactions). Where market evidence exceeds EUV+, market evidence is adopted (RICS FVP 2021 §3.5).
