Scoring Methodology

8-Axis Framework: Each dimension scored 0–100 from quantitative data:

AxisKey Drivers
Solar ResourceAnnual GHI (kWh/m²/yr), UK range benchmarking
Wind ResourceElevation-based proxy (terrain exposure)
TerrainMean slope (°), P90 slope, south-facing %
Land UseDevelopable area %, land class suitability
Grid AccessDistance to nearest substation (km), voltage tier
PlanningFlood risk, environmental constraints, land type
FinancialComposite: 35% solar + 30% grid + 20% terrain + 15% land
EnvironmentalWater occurrence, NDVI, flood risk level

Verdict: GO (≥65%), CAUTION (40–65%), NO-GO (<40%) of max possible score.
