Methodology Notes

Tier 1 (Data-driven): Substations within a 20 km search radius are identified, ranked by a composite suitability score incorporating headroom availability, distance, voltage level match, RAG status, and queue depth. Cost estimates use published UK DNO benchmark rates for cable, switchgear, transformer, and civils.

Cost Estimation: P10/P50/P90 range reflects uncertainty in reinforcement requirements, wayleave costs, and civils complexity. Cable costs are based on GRID_CONNECTION_COSTS_GBP_KM from CCC Carbon Budget 7 and validated against published DNO connection cost benchmarks.

Queue Analysis: Queue pressure is derived from the Embedded Capacity Register (ECR) and NESO TEC Register. Wait time estimates are heuristic and should be validated with DNO pre-application discussions.

{% if r.power_flow and r.power_flow.success %}

Tier 2 (Power Flow): A local network model is constructed from PostGIS substation and line data, solved using pandapower (Newton-Raphson solver, lightsim2grid backend). Voltage limits: ±6% at 11/33kV, ±5% at 66kV+ per Engineering Recommendations P28/P29. Thermal limits: 100% of continuous rating.

{% endif %}
