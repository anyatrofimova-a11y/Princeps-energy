## THE 15 MOST IMPORTANT FINDINGS

### 1. UK Grid Queue: 382 GW Post-Reform (Source: NESO Connections Reform Results, 2025)
The queue was 726 GW, NESO removed ~300 GW of "zombie" projects. 382 GW remains: 283 GW generation/storage + 99 GW demand. 132 GW aligned with Clean Power 2030 target.

**Princeps implication**: Every project in the 382 GW pipeline needs grid connection assessment. At £10-50k per assessment (consultant rates), the UK grid assessment TAM is £3.8-19 billion in aggregate. Princeps automates this for £50-100k/year subscription.

### 2. US Queue: 2,600 GW — 10,300 Projects (Source: LBNL Queued Up 2025 Edition)
The US interconnection queue contains 1,400 GW generation + 890 GW storage across 10,300 projects. Only 13% of projects submitted 2000-2019 reached commercial operation. Median time to connect has doubled from 2 to 4+ years.

**Princeps implication**: 87% project failure rate means massive waste in feasibility spending. US expansion would 10x the addressable market. FERC Order 2023 reform creates timing opportunity similar to UK Connections Reform.

### 3. Energy Management Software Market: $56 Billion (Source: Multiple market research firms, 2025)
The broader energy management software market is $56.2B in 2025, growing at 9.4% CAGR to $105B by 2032. The renewable energy asset management software sub-segment is $2.6B growing to $5B by 2035.

**Princeps implication**: Princeps spans multiple sub-segments (grid intelligence, feasibility, asset management, compliance). The specific addressable segment (pre-development intelligence) is estimated at $2-5B globally.

### 4. CompetitorRegIntel: $21M Series A at Pre-Revenue (Source: BusinessWire, March 2026)
CompetitorRegIntel raised $21M led by Energize Capital for energy regulatory NLP. US-only. No UK coverage, no GIS, no 3D, no design tools. Pure data subscription model.

**Princeps implication**: Validates the market. CompetitorRegIntel covers US regulatory NLP only — Princeps covers full lifecycle (site → design → compliance → operations) with real engineering tools, not just search. Princeps is further along in product but earlier in funding.

### 5. $850 Billion US Power Infrastructure Investment in 2026 (Source: CompetitorRegIntel Series A press release)
"Up to $850 billion will be invested in power and digital infrastructure in 2026" in the US alone, driving demand for intelligence tools.

**Princeps implication**: The investment wave is now. Developers need tools that work in months, not years. Timing is perfect for platform launch.

### 6. Demand Queue Explosion: 125 GW New UK Demand (Source: NESO, June 2025)
Since November 2024, 80 GW of new transmission demand connections + 5 GW distribution = 125 GW total demand queue. Data centres are a major driver.

**Princeps implication**: The DC-specific capabilities (15D scoring, CFE%, heat reuse, liquid cooling) directly serve this 125 GW demand pipeline. Google, Microsoft, Amazon are all in this queue.

### 7. UK Grid Constraint Costs Exceed £1.8 Billion/Year (Source: Power Technology, 2025)
Annual constraint payments to manage grid congestion reached £1.8B in the UK.

**Princeps implication**: Constraint forecast overlay (already built) helps developers avoid constrained zones. This saves real money — projects in constrained areas face curtailment risk.

### 8. Consultant Day Rates: £1,000-2,000/day for Grid Specialists (Source: Industry benchmarks)
Senior grid connection consultants at ARUP, WSP, Jacobs charge £1,000-2,000/day. A typical feasibility study takes 15-40 consultant days.

**Princeps implication**: A single feasibility study costs £15,000-80,000 in consultant time. Princeps delivers equivalent analysis in seconds. At £50-100k/year subscription, a developer saves money after 1-2 studies.

### 9. Only 13% of US Projects Reach Operation (Source: LBNL 2025)
77% of US interconnection requests are withdrawn. The feasibility → operation conversion rate is abysmal.

**Princeps implication**: Better upfront screening = fewer wasted applications. If Princeps can improve the success rate from 13% to even 20%, the economic value is billions in avoided waste.

### 10. PVcase/Anderson Optimization: Solar-Only, US-Focused (Source: pvcase.com)
PVcase Prospect acquired Anderson Optimization. Strong early-stage solar site selection but solar-only, primarily US data layers. Expanding to Europe.

**Princeps implication**: Princeps covers 5 asset types (solar, wind, BESS, DC, hydrogen) vs PVcase's 1. Princeps has real UK DNO data. PVcase is a feature, not a platform.

### 11. LandGate: 25TB Data, MCP-Compliant AI Agent (Source: LandGate press release, Feb 2026)
LandGate launched enterprise AI data agent. 25TB data, 60K substations mapped, 5,200 DCs. Model-agnostic (works with any LLM). Acquired Topos for consolidation.

**Princeps implication**: LandGate's MCP approach is validated — Princeps already has an MCP server. But LandGate is US-focused. No UK DNO data, no engineering analysis, no physics simulation.

### 12. Transect: Community Sentiment AI (Source: transect.com)
Transect assesses community opposition to solar projects using AI + ordinance data. Also offers interconnection capacity products.

**Princeps implication**: Princeps already has community sentiment predictor (dc_advanced_design.py). Transect is US-only. Princeps does this for UK with LPA-specific data.

### 13. Next UK Connection Window: H2 2026 (Source: NESO)
The next grid connection applications window opens in H2 2026 under the reformed process.

**Princeps implication**: Every developer preparing applications for H2 2026 needs gate readiness scoring — which Princeps provides. This is a time-sensitive sales window.

### 14. Electrical Asset Management Software: $10B Market (Source: Mordor Intelligence)
Expected to reach $10.13B in 2025, growing to $16.2B by 2030 at 9.73% CAGR.

**Princeps implication**: Asset lifecycle management (legacy compliance, condition monitoring, repowering analysis) is already built in Princeps. This is a separate revenue stream from pre-development.

### 15. No Single Platform Does What Princeps Does (Competitive Gap)
CompetitorRegIntel = US regulatory NLP only. PVcase = solar site selection. LandGate = data API. Transect = environmental screening. Searchland = land data. Ramboll = consulting. None offer: real-time UK grid data + physics simulation + 3D twin + AI agent + compliance + financial modelling + DC scoring in one platform.

**Princeps implication**: Princeps is the only full-lifecycle energy intelligence platform for the UK market. No direct competitor covers the full stack.
